## The challenge

The client managed customer projects across scattered spreadsheets and email threads, making status tracking difficult.

## What we built

A custom web portal where clients can view project status, upload files, and message the team directly.

## The result

Project status enquiries dropped significantly, and the team now has a single source of truth for every client.
