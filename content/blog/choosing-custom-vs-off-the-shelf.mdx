Off-the-shelf software is faster and cheaper to start with — but it isn't always the right long-term fit.

## When off-the-shelf works

If your process is fairly standard, a good SaaS tool will usually cover 80% of what you need.

## When to go custom

If you're bending your workflow to fit the software instead of the other way around, it's time to consider a custom build.
