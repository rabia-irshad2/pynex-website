Most small businesses overestimate the complexity of adopting AI. You don't need to build a model from scratch — you need to solve one specific problem well.

## Start with one process

Pick the single most repetitive, data-heavy task in your business and look at whether AI can meaningfully speed it up.

## Measure before you scale

Test on a small scale first, measure the impact, then expand once you know it works.
