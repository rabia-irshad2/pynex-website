## The challenge

Support requests were piling up faster than the small support team could respond, with most questions being repetitive.

## What we built

A WhatsApp-based AI assistant that answers common questions instantly and routes complex issues to a human agent.

## The result

First-response time dropped from hours to seconds, and the support team now focuses only on complex cases.
