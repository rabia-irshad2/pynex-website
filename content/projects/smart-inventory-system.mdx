## The challenge

The client's warehouse team was tracking stock levels manually across spreadsheets, leading to frequent stockouts and overstock.

## What we built

An automated inventory system that syncs stock levels in real time across warehouse and sales channels, with automatic reorder alerts.

## The result

Manual stock checks dropped by 80%, and stockout incidents fell to near zero within the first quarter.
