Business automation doesn't mean replacing your team — it means removing the repetitive, low-value work that eats up their time.

## Where automation usually starts

Most businesses start with the tasks that are repetitive, rule-based, and time-consuming: data entry, status updates, and report generation.

## How to know if you need it

If your team is doing the same manual task every week with no real judgment involved, it's a strong automation candidate.
